Venus

Earth's blistering twin

Similar in size to Earth but wrapped in a crushing carbon-dioxide atmosphere, Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system — hot enough to melt lead. A runaway greenhouse effect keeps its surface at a furnace-like 465°C, and it spins backwards, so on Venus the Sun rises in the west.

  • Distance from Sun108.2 million km
  • Day length243 Earth days
  • Year length225 Earth days
  • Diameter12,104 km
  • Surface temp465°C
