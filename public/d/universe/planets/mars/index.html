Mars

The red frontier

Rusty iron dust gives Mars its colour. It hosts the tallest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, and a canyon system that would stretch across the United States. Dry riverbeds and polar ice hint at a wetter past — which is why it's the prime target in the search for past life.
