Mercury

The swift, sun-scorched world

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun, Mercury races around our star in just 88 days. With almost no atmosphere to trap heat, it swings from 430°C in daylight to −180°C at night — the most extreme temperature range of any planet.

  • Distance from Sun57.9 million km
  • Day length59 Earth days
  • Year length88 Earth days
  • Diameter4,879 km
  • Moons0
