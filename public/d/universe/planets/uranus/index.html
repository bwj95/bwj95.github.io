Uranus

The tilted ice giant

Uranus is tipped over on its side, likely knocked askew by an ancient collision, so it rolls around the Sun like a ball rather than spinning like a top. That means each pole gets 42 years of continuous sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness. Methane in its atmosphere gives it a pale cyan glow.

  • Distance from Sun2.87 billion km
  • Day length17.2 hours
  • Year length84 Earth years
  • Diameter50,724 km
  • Moons28
