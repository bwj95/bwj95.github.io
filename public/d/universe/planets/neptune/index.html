Neptune

The windswept blue giant

The most distant planet, so far out that a single orbit takes 165 years — it has completed just one full lap since its discovery in 1846. Neptune whips up the fastest winds in the solar system, screaming past at over 2,000 km/h, and was the first planet found by mathematics before anyone saw it.

  • Distance from Sun4.5 billion km
  • Day length16.1 hours
  • Year length165 Earth years
  • Diameter49,244 km
  • Moons16
