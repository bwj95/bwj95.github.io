Saturn

The jewel of the solar system

Famous for its dazzling rings — billions of chunks of ice and rock, some as small as dust, some as big as houses — Saturn is the second-largest planet yet so light it would float in water. Its moon Titan has lakes of liquid methane and a thick atmosphere all its own.

  • Distance from Sun1.43 billion km
  • Day length10.7 hours
  • Year length29.5 Earth years
  • Diameter116,460 km
  • Moons146+
