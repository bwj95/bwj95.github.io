Earth

Our pale blue dot

The only world known to harbour life, Earth sits in the Sun's habitable zone where liquid water can pool on the surface. Seventy-one percent of it is ocean, and a single large Moon steadies its axial tilt — keeping the seasons, and the climate, remarkably stable.
