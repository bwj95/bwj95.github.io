Jupiter

King of the planets

A gas giant so massive it could swallow every other planet with room to spare. Its Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has raged for centuries. Jupiter spins faster than any other planet — a day lasts under 10 hours — and its gravity shields the inner planets from stray comets.

  • Distance from Sun778.5 million km
  • Day length9.9 hours
  • Year length11.9 Earth years
  • Diameter139,820 km
  • Moons95+
