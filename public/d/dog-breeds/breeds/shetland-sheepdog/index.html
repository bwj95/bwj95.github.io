About the Shetland Sheepdog

Developed on Scotland's rugged Shetland Islands to guard crofts and herd small livestock, the 'Sheltie' is a sensitive, highly trainable little dog. It excels at obedience and agility, bonds closely with family, and tends to be a vocal watchdog.
