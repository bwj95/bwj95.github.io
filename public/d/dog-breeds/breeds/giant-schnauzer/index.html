About the Giant Schnauzer

Originally a cattle drover and brewery guard in Bavaria, the Giant Schnauzer is a powerful, intelligent, high-energy working dog now used in police and protection roles. It needs a job, firm training, and plenty of exercise to be a balanced companion.
