About the Australian Cattle Dog

Created to move cattle over vast Australian distances, the Cattle Dog (or Blue/Red Heeler) is hardy, driven, and fiercely loyal to its person. It needs a serious outlet for its energy and intelligence, and forms an intense one-owner bond.
