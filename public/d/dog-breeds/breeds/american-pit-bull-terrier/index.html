About the American Pit Bull Terrier

Bred from bulldogs and terriers, the Pit Bull is a strong, athletic, and famously people-oriented dog that historically earned the 'nanny dog' nickname for its gentleness with children. Loyal and eager to please, it thrives with training, exercise, and responsible ownership.
