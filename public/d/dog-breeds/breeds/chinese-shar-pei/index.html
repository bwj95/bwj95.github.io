About the Chinese Shar-Pei

An ancient Chinese breed once used for guarding and hunting, the Shar-Pei is famous for its loose, wrinkled skin, 'hippopotamus' muzzle, and blue-black tongue. Calm, aloof, and intensely loyal to its family, it is reserved with strangers and strong-willed.
