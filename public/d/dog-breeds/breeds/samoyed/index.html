About the Samoyed

Bred by the nomadic Samoyede people to herd reindeer and sleep among the family for warmth, this fluffy spitz is sociable to its core. Its upturned 'Sammy smile' matches a cheerful, affectionate personality — and its coat sheds prodigiously.
