About the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

Named for the Stuart kings who adored the breed, the Cavalier is a sweet, graceful toy spaniel that wants nothing more than to be close to its people. Adaptable and universally friendly, it suits almost any home from flat to farmhouse.
