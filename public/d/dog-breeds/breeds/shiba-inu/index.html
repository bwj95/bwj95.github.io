About the Shiba Inu

Japan's most popular companion dog, the Shiba is an ancient, fox-faced hunting breed known for its spirited, self-possessed nature. Clean and independent almost to a fault, it bonds deeply yet keeps its own counsel — and is a famous escape artist.
