About the Lhasa Apso

For over a thousand years the Lhasa Apso served as an indoor sentinel in Tibetan monasteries and palaces, alerting monks to intruders. Hardy and self-assured despite its small size, it's an affectionate but discerning companion under a long, flowing coat.
